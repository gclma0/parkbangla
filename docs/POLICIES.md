# ParkBangla Policy Drafts

These drafts are placeholders for legal review before public launch.

## Privacy Policy

ParkBangla collects account details, phone number, vehicle details, booking activity, approximate and precise parking coordinates, uploaded listing proof, messages, reports, and wallet ledger records needed to run the parking marketplace.

Precise location and identity document data should be used only for booking, trust, fraud prevention, support, and legal compliance. Access should be limited to the user, relevant booking participant, and authorized operations staff.

Users can request account support through the safety/support flow. Production launch should add a formal data deletion and retention request process.

## Terms Of Service

Users must provide accurate account, vehicle, listing, access, and payment information. Hosts may list only parking spaces they own or are authorized to offer. Renters must use spaces only during their confirmed booking window.

ParkBangla may restrict, suspend, or remove accounts or listings for unsafe behavior, harassment, misleading listings, repeated cancellations, payment abuse, or fraud.

## Cancellation Policy

Prototype cancellation currently refunds 80% of the held booking amount when a booking is cancelled through the app. Admin manual adjustments may override this during support resolution and must be auditable.

Production launch should define time-based cancellation windows, no-show rules, overstay fees, late checkout fees, and provider-backed refund timing.

## Host Agreement

Hosts are responsible for accurate location, entrance instructions, pricing, availability, photos, and permission proof. Hosts must keep availability current and avoid cancelling confirmed bookings except where access or safety requires it.

Repeated host cancellation, misleading access instructions, or duplicate listings may trigger review, deactivation, or account restrictions.

## Data Retention

Suggested defaults before launch:

- Account and wallet ledger records: retain while account is active and as legally required.
- Booking records: retain for support, tax, and dispute windows.
- Chat/report/dispute evidence: retain through the dispute window, then archive or delete according to legal advice.
- Precise location history: do not store beyond what is needed for the booking/listing record.
- Uploaded KYC/listing proof: restrict access and define deletion timing after verification or account closure.
